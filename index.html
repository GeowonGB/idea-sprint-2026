Why 24 hours

Twenty-four hours is enough to turn a rough idea into something real.

  • Ship over slides.A working demo beats a perfect deck.
  • Fail early, fix fast.A broken prototype teaches more than a polished plan.
  • Show your work.Pitch what you built, not what you plan to build.
